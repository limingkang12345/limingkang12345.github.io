Use context to understand new words

（阅读微技能：根据上下文理解新词）

Compare to looking up the new words in a dictionary while we come across new words in reading, using context to understand the new words is better.

How to: Find Clues.

Clues: Includes word clue, sentence clue.

  1. Word clue: Synonym(近义词), antonym(反义词), the connection between the words.

  2. Sentence clue: To catch sentence clues, we need to find the “sign word“, which may delicate the contrast, examples or explanation.
